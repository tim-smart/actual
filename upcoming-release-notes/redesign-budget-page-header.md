Redesign the budget page header: month headings sit over their columns, the first naming its year, with a pager in the corner (a step button at each end and Today between them) and a calendar button beside it that opens a month grid, which replaces the month strip and selects every month shown as one band, a single account menu replaces the toolbar icons, and each month's "to budget" figure opens its breakdown and actions. The account menu is one control, the avatar with the user's initials and a menu chevron, framed like the header's other controls and always the same width, whose dot, tooltip, menu header and sync item read one sync status (no server, sync failed, server unreachable, local-only file, sync turned off, syncing, synced). The budget header starts at the top of the page, in the browser and in the desktop app (whose window frame holds its title bar and controls), with the account avatar at its corner and a compact uncategorized-transactions count beside it on its heading row, clear of the last month's own buttons; the account menu's dot is amber when the server cannot be reached. The month count is a framed control under the pager, like the calendar button beside it, level with the months' figures, that says "3 months" and opens the counts to choose from. The summary row's height is chosen from every month in the budget, so it never changes while stepping, the months on screen always share one layout, a figure's amount is set a size smaller before its details go under it, and month columns are sized from the widest amount as the budget formats it. Each month's heading and figure read as one band inside the header's frame: the header keeps the same air over the month names as at its sides, the name sits close over its figure, and the current month's name begins with a dot, as a calendar marks today, so every heading sits on the same band. Where the budget's first or last month is on screen, a caption under the corner's controls says so. Each month's figure is set large in its state's colour on the month itself, with its label on a small tinted chip under it that is the button for the month's actions (a tracking figure, which opens nothing, has a plain label), and the amounts beside it are a small block, labels in one column and their values starting together in the next, each value an amount with its plan after it where there is one, so every label meets its value after one gap and a line reads as one phrase, running from the figure's top at one pitch a little air after the figure's column, with nothing drawn between, that is one block across the months: it starts after the budget's widest figure in every month, the block's columns are the budget's widest, and a month with fewer lines leaves the room under them empty; it reads the same way at every width, and the figure is set a size smaller before the block moves under it. A tracking month's ledger, its income and spending each of its plan, sits under the figure at every width, the same block in the same place however many months are shown. Tracking budgets headline every month's savings, say when a month's plan is missing its income or spending, keep the Spent line as the total and say how far spending is over plan on a line of its own under Spent, "Over plan 1,132.88", in amber while the month is under way, when the planned savings amount takes the same amber (as the envelope figure takes its state's colour) and its label stays plain, and in red once it is settled, and label a month of net refunds as such; a line beside a figure that says a plan is missing shows its amount alone, and a month with nothing planned and nothing in shows only its label, no lines of nothing. Menus expose menu item roles, move keyboard focus onto the item they highlight and choose the focused item from the keyboard whatever the pointer is over; Shift with the arrow keys moves a year at a time. A month's note button shows when it has a note; otherwise "Add note" is in its menu.
